01 — Polling & rankings

There is exactly one published scientific poll for this race, and it comes with major caveats. It is shown below because it is the only polling data that exists — not because it is a forecast.

Read this first. The Pallas Data poll (fielded June 24–28, 2026; published July 15) was commissioned by the Conservative Electors Association, a participant in this election, and ran before nominations closed on September 11. It therefore does not describe the final ballot. Almost half the sample (48.4%) was undecided — a larger share than any candidate received. Margin of error ±5.6 pts (95% CI), n=304.
